# DOT CLOCK — privacy information

Publisher: ABE

Support: abdullam.09@gmail.com

DOT CLOCK reads the computer clock and the settings supplied by iCUE to display the time, date, and chosen appearance. Optional background images are loaded locally on the user's computer. The widget does not transmit these values or images to ABE or another service.

The widget includes no analytics, telemetry, advertising, accounts, remote fonts, or external API calls. It does not independently store personal information or read hardware sensors. iCUE manages widget settings under its own behavior and policies. Marketplace purchases and any information voluntarily sent to the support email are separate from the widget's operation.
