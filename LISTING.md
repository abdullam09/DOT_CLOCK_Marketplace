## Description — copy into Maker Console

DOT CLOCK is a customizable dot-matrix digital clock for CORSAIR XENEON EDGE. Read local time or a world time zone at a glance, with optional seconds and a locale-aware date. Its minimal dot design pairs an off-white clock with red accents on black.

Choose 12- or 24-hour time, round or square dots, dot spacing, blinking separators, inactive dots, colors, and a local background image. Brightness, blur, and outer-background transparency controls let you personalize the display. Solid backing and automatic contrast correction keep the clock and labels readable.

Layouts adapt to Small, Medium, Large, and Extra Large slots in horizontal and vertical orientations. Requires Windows and iCUE 5.47 or later. Settings labels are in English; dates follow the iCUE language. No accounts, API keys, plugins, internet connection, telemetry, or companion software are required.
